<p align="center"><i>An addon by Pocky for the <a href="https://www.curseforge.com/members/satisfy/projects">Let's Do</a> mods by satisfy: work at their stations with your own hands.</i></p>

<p align="center"><b>The fermentation barrel, the apple press, the cooking pot, the stove, the roaster, the mincer, the crafting bowl and the tea kettle open right where they stand, with no menu screen. You stir the soup, turn the mincer's crank and whisk the dough yourself, and players nearby see what you make.</b></p>

<p align="center"><img src="docs/showcase/cooking_pot.gif" alt="Water and barley go into the cooking pot on the stove, then the soup is stirred with the pot's stick until it is done" width="720"></p>

<p align="center"><b>No menus</b>, the blocks open in the world &middot; <b>Every recipe</b> of the Let's Do mods &middot; <b>No new blocks or items</b></p>

Built on [Diegesis](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/diegesis), from the maker of
[Hands-On Delight](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/hands-on-delight),
[Hands-On Vanilla](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/hands-on-vanilla) and
[Hands-On Furniture](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/hands-on-furniture).

## Features

- **Fermentation barrel** ([Vinery](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-vinery)). Take the lid off, pour
  the juice in and drop the ingredients in. With the lid back on everything ferments together, and the wine waits in the
  barrel until you draw it at the bung into bottles.
- **Apple press** (Vinery). Open the basket, lay the apples in and press the screw's cap. The juice runs into a small tub
  under the press, and wine bottles are filled from the tub.
- **Cooking pot** ([Farm & Charm](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-farm-charm)). Throw the food into
  the water and stir it with the pot's stick. Wide, steady rounds earn stars that add servings; bowls dipped into the soup
  take it out.
- **Stove** (Farm & Charm). Open the door, slide the grate out and lay the bread or the roast on it, coal goes into the
  firebox. It bakes with the door shut.
- **Roaster** (Farm & Charm). Lay the food into the pan and swirl the roaster by its handle while it browns in its fat.
- **Mincer** (Farm & Charm). Drop the meat into the hopper and turn the crank by hand, up to eight pieces at once. A
  heavier load turns slower, and the mince waits under the spout.
- **Crafting bowl** (Farm & Charm). Pour the milk in, add the rest and whisk it with the bowl's own whisk until the whole
  bowlful is porridge, dough or butter.
- **Tea kettle** ([HerbalBrews](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-herbal-brews)). Lift the lid, pour
  water in and drop the leaves in. The kettle whistles when the tea is done and is poured from its spout.
- **The badge beside every station** shows what the next step needs: the bowl or bottle to take the result in, the fuel or
  heat that is missing, or the tool your hand should take.
- **Nothing breaks.** The stations work on the blocks' own storage: hoppers keep working, the result is made by the Let's Do
  mod itself, and players without this mod keep the Let's Do menus.

## Controls

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Open a station | Right-click the block |
| Put a stack in | Click it in your inventory, then the station |
| Take something back | Click it at the station |
| Stir, crank, whisk, press | Take the stick, crank, whisk or cap and move the mouse |
| Fill a bowl or a bottle | Click the soup, the tub, the bung or the spout with it |
| Leave | Escape or the inventory key |

## Configuration

`config/handson_letsdo.properties` turns each station off (its block then keeps its Let's Do menu), turns off the
stirring the pot waits for, and scales the time of the pot and the roaster.

## Installation

1. Minecraft 1.20.1 with [Forge](https://files.minecraftforge.net), Minecraft 1.21.1 with
   [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net), or either of them with [Fabric](https://fabricmc.net) and
   [Fabric API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fabric-api).
2. [Diegesis](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/diegesis), this mod and the Let's Do mods you play with in the
   `mods` folder of the server and the client. Each Let's Do mod is optional and brings its own stations.

Bug reports, questions and ideas for new stations are welcome in [Pocky's Kitchen](https://discord.gg/mGkPKccJEf), our
Discord server.

## Building

```sh
./gradlew build
```

The jars are written to `forge/build/libs/` and `fabric/build/libs/` (on the `1.21.1` branch `neoforge/` and `fabric/`).
