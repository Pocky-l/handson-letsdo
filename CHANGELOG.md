# Changelog

All notable changes to this mod are documented in this file.
The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.2.0+1.21.1] - Unreleased
### Added
- [Candlelight](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-candlelight-farm-charm-compat)'s eleven stoves are stations: open the door, lay the food on the grate and shut it to bake, as at [Farm & Charm](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-farm-charm)'s stove.

## [1.1.0+1.21.1] - 2026-11-26
### Added
- [Candlelight](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-candlelight-farm-charm-compat)'s cooking pot is a station: stir it as [Farm & Charm](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-farm-charm)'s pot for stars that add servings, and dip bowls into the soup.
- [Candlelight](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-candlelight-farm-charm-compat)'s cooking pan is a station: lay the food in and swirl it by its long handle as [Farm & Charm](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-farm-charm)'s roaster for stars that add servings. What fries in the open pan shows in the world too.
### Changed
- Opening a station with an item in hand no longer puts that item on the cursor: it stays in its slot.

## [1.0.1+1.21.1] - 2026-11-25
### Fixed
- The cooking pot and the tea kettles showed broken textures with Farm & Charm and HerbalBrews for 1.21.1: they now look as those mods draw them.

## [1.0.0+1.21.1] - 2026-11-25
### Added
- [Vinery](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-vinery)'s fermentation barrel is a station: everything in it ferments together with the lid on, and the wine is drawn at the bung.
- Vinery's apple press is a station: lay the apples in, press the screw's cap by hand and fill bottles from the tub under it.
- [Farm & Charm](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-farm-charm)'s cooking pot and roaster are stations: stir the pot or swirl the pan, steady rounds earn stars that add servings.
- Farm & Charm's stove is a station: lay the dough on its grate, coal in the firebox, and it bakes with the door shut.
- Farm & Charm's mincer and crafting bowl are stations: work the crank or the whisk by hand, up to eight pieces or a whole bowlful at once.
- [HerbalBrews](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-herbal-brews)' tea kettles are a station: pour the water in, drop the leaves in and pour the tea from the spout.
- A badge beside every station shows what the next step needs: a bowl or bottle, the missing fuel or heat, or the tool for your hand.
