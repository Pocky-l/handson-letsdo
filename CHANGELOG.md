## [1.0.0+1.20.1] - Unreleased

### Added
- [Vinery](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-vinery)'s fermentation barrel is a station: everything in it ferments together with the lid on, and the wine is drawn at the bung.
- Vinery's apple press is a station: lay the apples in, press the screw's cap by hand and fill bottles from the tub under it.
- [Farm & Charm](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-farm-charm)'s cooking pot and roaster are stations: stir the pot or swirl the pan, steady rounds earn stars that add servings.
- Farm & Charm's stove is a station: lay the dough on its grate, coal in the firebox, and it bakes with the door shut.
- Farm & Charm's mincer and crafting bowl are stations: work the crank or the whisk by hand, up to eight pieces or a whole bowlful at once.
- [HerbalBrews](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-herbal-brews)' tea kettles are a station: pour the water in, drop the leaves in and pour the tea from the spout.
- A badge beside every station shows what the next step needs: a bowl or bottle, the missing fuel or heat, or the tool for your hand.
